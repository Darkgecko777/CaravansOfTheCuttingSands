# Naming — phonetic guidelines

**Status:** Cleaned this session. Sound rules unchanged.  
**Use:** New places, clerks, string tokens, road voices. Not a language.

Dark Sun is not the model. Hyborian proper nouns are not the source. The mouth-feel is a Savage Sword name: short, one breath, hard, the same kit every time. A layperson should hear that a new clerk belongs on this map. They should not be able to decline a verb.

## Two registers

**Old tongue.** Seats, villages, clans, people.

**Road English.** Wagon names on some posts: Westmark, Highmark, Southmark, Brineford, Ridgewatch. Legal for a post. Illegal for a person or a village. Kaleth is old tongue and may sit on a post. Do not add more wagon-names of that kind unless the post is deliberately one. The ending on those three posts means march in the world language. It is not a system word.

## Shape

- Say it once, aloud, before it ships.
- People: one or two syllables. One syllable is a clan name or a hard given name. Two is the normal clerk.
- Places: two syllables. Three only for a seat-scale name, still stressed on the first beat.
- Stress the first syllable. No trailing lilt.
- No apostrophes, no hyphens in old tongue, no internal capitals.

## Sounds

Onsets and clusters: k, kh, g, gh, t, th, d, n, m, r, s, sh, z, v, l.

Vowels: a, e, o, u, i. The circumflex û exists on Kharûn and Ghûl only. Do not add a second diacritic, and do not put û on new names.

Good joints: khar, ghor, thul, vek, rukh, neth, mor, kal, drav, tor, ash, zam.

Do not use:

- Endings: -iel, -wen, -elle, -ara, -ien, -ion, -ynne, -ya, -ae.
- Starts: ae, iae, ela, syl, lyr, any name that needs a silent letter.
- Soft stacks: ph, qu, wh, a doubled l that is only there to look elvish.
- Hyborian or Dark Sun proper nouns as ours. Zamora, Shem, Tyr, and Balic are the smell, not the list.
- A settlement name, or that name with a vowel swapped, as a person. Gharun, Rukha, Khorath, Gorath, Kethar, and Nethuli are the map misheard.

Sorel is the soft leak on the current map. Draven is legal as a place and pairs with Torvern. It is also a stock given name elsewhere. Do not hand it to a person. The place is Torvern, not Torven.

## Places

Reuse these endings before inventing one.

| Ending | Already on the map | Use |
|---|---|---|
| -ath | Zamath, Ghorath | Seat, old place |
| -un | Kharûn | Seat, rare |
| -or / -ar | Thalor, Ashar | Ridge, city, high ground |
| -thar | Veythar | Seat, do not multiply |
| -en / -vern | Draven, Torvern | Foothill, road village |
| -eth | Kaleth | Post or village |
| one beat | Ghûl, Rukh, Neth, Sarn | Clan |

Moraq is the only q. One q on the map is enough.

Places are not gendered. A village may end on a vowel (Kethra) or a consonant (Draven). That split is for people.

## People

Houses take their names from the cities. Kharûn the house is named for Kharûn the city, not the other way around. Blood-tie to a house is a small subset of the people who live there. Most residents are of the city. They are not of the house.

No inherited surname. A given name, then an identifier when the yard needs one.

- Parent: son of Korv, daughter of Vekra.
- Place: of Draven, of Kharûn. The city or village, not the house, unless the speaker is naming house kin.
- Vocation: the factor, the clerk, the wrangler.

Use one or two. Not all three every time. House kin is "of House Kharûn" and is rare. Shore people may use the clan place: of Rukh, of Neth, of Ghûl.

**Gender, locked.** Men end on a consonant. Women end on a vowel. Same onsets. A woman's name is not a softened man's name with a flower on it, and it is not an elf name. -eth and -ath are place endings, not women's names.

The tutorial clerk is fixed: **Vekra of Kharûn, the clerk.** Everyone else is drawn once per save from `Name_List.md` and not reused in that save. The first build does not need the whole reservoir.

## Map, do not quietly rename

Seats: Kharûn, Zamath, Thalor, Veythar, Ghorath.

Villages: Ghûl, Rukh, Neth, Kethra, Sorel, Draven, Moraq, Torvern, Ashar.

Posts: Westmark, Kaleth, Highmark, Southmark, Brineford, Ridgewatch.

Sarn's Rest stays a landmark. Sarn is the old-tongue piece.

## Test

Read a new name next to Kethra and Rukh. If it sounds like a different book, cut it. If it sounds like the map, cut it. If a man's name ends on a vowel, or a woman's on a consonant, cut it. If it is three syllables, cut it.
