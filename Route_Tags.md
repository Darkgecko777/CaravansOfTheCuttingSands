# Route Tags Chart

Tags gate which encounter rows can be drawn on that road.

Wildlife-desert, bandits, and catchall are on every road.
A city tag is on every road that touches that city.
Wildlife-sea and wilderness-mountain are only on the roads listed with those tags.

The column records the tags. Play derives wildlife-desert, bandits, catchall, and the city from the road's ends. It reads wildlife-sea and wilderness-mountain from this column.
