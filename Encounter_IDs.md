# Encounter ID Chart

The chart stores each id, its category, and day, night, or either. Titles of the written cards are listed here. Scenes, choices, and effects are in `data/config/encounter_cards.json`.

Unwritten rows stay blank. They are not drawn until a card with a road pool is written for that id.

Ancient mysteries are expedition-only and never drawn on ordinary road checks.
Rival-house rows stay out of the road pool until a later pass can tell that the leg crossed a caravan of a house.
BA-04 and BA-05 are retired. The rows stay. They are never drawn.
FU-01 is scripted and is never drawn from a road roll.
CA-06 through CA-11 are in the catch-all road pool. The Hermit, CA-11, is drawn at half weight.
