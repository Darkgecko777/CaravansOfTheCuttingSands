# Caravans of the Cutting Sands — Goods

Names are locked. Bulk, look, and price are a working pass. This is not a price list and not a packing sheet.

Drawn from the artist brief. Location stories are not in this file. Tag them: Lore to be added in future update.

Do not add origin goods for scrubstone, for posts, or for Sarn’s Rest. Dream Lotus Nectar is never on a legal market board.

## Bulk, as a feel

Not locked numbers.

- One grip: feathers, pearls, nectar, wick, talons, gems, a rod, a fruit.
- A pile or a two-hand bundle: jars, tools, pottery, tubers, hides, salt, cured meat, rope.
- Awkward: living lumber and Hulv bone want space and a second person.
- Dense: small, and heavy for the size. Frost iron, raw shimmersteel, Titan’s Heart-rock, brine salt.
- Light for the look: Palestone looks like stone and is not. Fineware looks like metal and is a small precious set, not a crate of bars.

## On every market

**Water.** Fresh water. Cities and villages sell it. The container looks local. The traveller’s canteen is kit, not cargo: a large waterskin, about a hundred litres as story-capacity, drunk by the watch. Cargo water can be tipped into it at a poor rate.

**Rations.** Cooked travel food, not an origin good. Hard bread, dried stew, pressed cake. Edible origin goods can be broken into rations at a bad rate. Do not draw rations as those goods.

Edible on the current sheet: Tiger Cactus Flesh, Moon Soaked Zethi-Bulb, Finger Fungus, Fire Salt, Sweetrock, Clack Fiend Larvae, Kel Eel Flesh. Sweet Bliss is food until it turns toxic.

## Shimmersteel

One metal, mined only at Thalor. Work changes the finish. It stands in for bronze, silver, and gold depending on the grade. It is not coin.

Raw bars leave Thalor. Kharûn works the lowest grade into plain resilient jars. Thalor works a practical middle grade into cookware. Veythar does not mine it and works the highest grade into fineware. Four finishes, one family.

## Cities

Four origin goods each. House seat. The house is named for the city.

### Kharûn

Scrubstone comes out of the ground here and becomes coin. The city does not mine shimmersteel. It buys organ oil from Rukh.

- **Shimmersteel Jars.** Lowest grade, basic resilient jars. A crate, a bit heavy for the space. Plain, thick-walled, meant to survive the road.
- **Tiger Cactus Flesh.** Cut flesh of a carnivorous plant that moves. Edible. A modest cut, ordinary heft, meat more than salad.
- **Khor Crafted Tools.** A bundle of practical tools. Solid, not precious. Exact faces not fixed.
- **Scrubcast Resin.** Byproduct of pulling scrubstone. A pot or cake. Industrial, sticky. Not the coin.

### Ghorath

Oasis fortress. Fresh water is the ordinary good. Named plants are the export. Other oasis growth is not a trade good.

- **Dream Lotus Nectar.** Mind-altering, not a rumour. A small vial, very light, precious. Leaves the city only by smuggling. Not a legal market good.
- **Crimson Eyed Melon Juice.** Hallucinogenic hookah juice, legal. A flask, light.
- **Numbspindle.** Potent anesthetic. Medicine in the city, and the hunting tool Ghûl uses on the hulv. A small bundle, light.
- **Mor Root.** Plant fibre sold as lamp wick. A coil, very light.

### Veythar

Terraced farms on mountain spring water. Building stock is rock. No metal of its own. Works other people’s shimmersteel into the highest grade.

- **Moon Soaked Zethi-Bulb.** Edible terrace tuber. A sack, ordinary farm heft.
- **Titan’s Heart-rock.** Forge rock for very high heat. Same space as a sack of tubers, much heavier. Dense, dark, heat-scarred. Do not paint titans on it.
- **Shimmersteel Fineware.** Highest grade. A small precious set: cups, a bowl, a dish. Light for metal.
- **Vek-kilned Pottery.** Intricately worked ceramics. A crate, ordinary clay heft.

### Thalor

The shimmersteel mine, and the door for Ishka goods. Ishka are nomads from the uncharted dunes north of the map. Their goods also turn up at Highmark, where they come for Draven fungus. Highmark does not produce them.

- **Shimmersteel.** Raw bars or blooms, heavy. Mine grit, not a polished surface.
- **Shimmersteel Cookware.** Middle grade. A pot or a small set, ordinary heft. Road kitchen.
- **Giant Duzhu Hide.** Ishka product, exported here. A rolled hide, bulky more than dense. The giant of the starter mount: eight-legged reptile, oversized.
- **Ishka Crafted Music Makers.** Instruments, exported through Thalor. Awkward bundle, not lead-heavy. Strings, drums, or wind still open.

### Zamath

Shore city. Trees here are sentient. Speakers debate them for fruit and lumber. Cutting them down has gone badly. A portion of the city is the Neth embassy. Neth itself is opened from here, with no travel time.

- **Living Lumber.** Timber from those trees. A beam, two-person, the largest ordinary cargo. Still wood that was argued with, not a corpse-log. How alive it should look is open.
- **Water Witching Rods.** Rods from that lumber, used to find water. A single rod, light. Draven imports them often.
- **Sweet Bliss Pomegranates.** Fruit of the same trees. Sweet and euphoric. A handful, light. Decays fast and turns toxic. Draw the fresh fruit. Spoiled state later.
- **Brine Scrub.** Industrial salt from desalting sea water. A heavy sack. Not Sorel’s edible salts.

## Villages

Two origin goods each. Market and plaza only.

### Ashar

Mountain village. Stone, not metal. Terrorshrieks are oversized birds of prey in the ranges.

- **Palestone Bricks.** Light rigid stone, building material. Looks like stone, lifts like wood. Pale, glassy edges.
- **Terrorshriek Talons.** A few in a wrap, light, dangerous.

### Moraq

Dune village. Two luxury goods. Pays well for water, including brine.

- **Dune Mollusk Pearls.** A pouch, very light. Desert shell, not a sea oyster unless that changes.
- **Sunburnt Brew.** Liquor from the fermented guts of the same mollusks. A bottle, light to ordinary.

### Torvern

Kelkris eat the molt and leavings of pincer beasts. Pincer beasts are the second pack tier. No pack system yet.

- **Kelkris Feathers.** A light bundle, almost no heft. Feathered rodent, not a bird.
- **Pincer Beast Fur.** A pelt roll, bulky more than dense.

### Draven

Highland village. Caves around it. Imports water witching rods from Zamath and shimmersteel jars from Kharûn. A small mine, not the shimmersteel seat.

- **Frost Iron.** Small quantity. Cold to the touch, always. A small bar, surprisingly heavy. Not shimmersteel.
- **Finger Fungus.** Cave growth only. Blooms look like hands. Edible. A cluster, light. Ishka at Highmark buy these.

### Sorel

Salt flats, south of the main map. Both goods are edible salts. Not Zamath’s industrial brine scrub.

- **Fire Salt.** Spicy edible salt. A cake or pouch, ordinary salt heft.
- **Sweetrock.** Sweet edible salt. Same heft, different face. A market should be able to show both.

### Kethra

Both goods come off clack fiends, the insects.

- **Clack Fiend Larvae.** Large insect eggs. Edible. A crate, ordinary heft.
- **Clack Fiend Molt-skin.** Nutrient-rich shed. A skin or sheet, light. Not a hide from a kill.

### Neth

Sea village. No road leg. The player opens it from Zamath. Cult and shore detail: Lore to be added in future update.

- **Eyes of Arkhul.** Gizzard gems. A pouch, very light, luxury. Named for a sacred figure, taken from the gut of what they worship. Not costume jewellery.
- **Reth’s Blood.** Gland ink. A vial, light.

### Rukh

Sea village. The player opens it from Westmark, no hop. Kharûn imports the oil. Sarn’s Rest is a landmark, not a dock.

- **Sarn’s Bane Liver.** Organ oil from the Sarn’s Bane. A cask, solid heft. The good Kharûn’s markets ask for.
- **Kel Eel Flesh.** Brine meat. Edible, cures easily. Sides or a crate, solid heft. Breaks into rations at the bad rate.

### Ghûl

East of Southmark. A normal hop, unlike Neth and Rukh.

- **Zar’Kun Sinew.** Tentacle rope from the non-sentient larval form. A coil, bulky more than dense.
- **Hulv Bone.** Massive bone from a shore-grazer, construction stock. A beam, the other awkward good beside living lumber, and heavier. Hunted with numbspindle.

## Still open

Forms not fixed: Ishka instruments, the face of Khor tools, how living the lumber should read, spoiled pomegranate, nectar vial against an ordinary jar.

Price, pack size, and weight numbers are not locked. The bulk words above are the feel until they are.
