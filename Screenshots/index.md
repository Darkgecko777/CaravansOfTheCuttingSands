The encounter card shows a title, a scene, and two choices. It is 33_encounter.png. The camp card and the after-camp pace card are 34_camp.png and 35_resume_pace.png. Play has no tooltip type and no separate dialog type.

21_plaza_gates.png is the gates with no road lit. 30_travel_map.png is those same gates with the Kharûn to Draven road lit. That gates view is the travel map the player opens, and the neighbour road buttons stay on its panel.

31_road_choice.png is the gates panel with the Draven road lit. Careful, Steady, and Rush are on that panel, and Embark leaves. No card covers the map. The pace card opens after camp, which is 35_resume_pace.png, in the order Careful, Steady, Rush.

House and Stables stay off the layout while ShowUnfinishedFeatures is off. The Tavern is on at Kharûn and sells the six pieces of Sarn's map, gated by House Kharûn rank. Leads discount the next piece. The logbook lists owned pieces. All six open the Westmark road to Sarn's Rest. The run ends on the Embarked ledger. Gear stays on the top bar and opens the pause menu, which is part of play. The title menu is a compact wood panel under the tagline. The title, title Options, and the pause menu use that wood panel and text-only items, with a bone tick and no row bars. Title Options includes Show intro again.

03_intro_card.png is the first-run intro card, one wood panel over the Kharûn market. It shows the header, the body, the tagline, and a text-only BEGIN. Play input is locked. Later New Games skip the card unless Show intro again is on.

15_market_ridgewatch.png is the long dock name. Sarn's Rest is a landmark. House and Stables are off the bar in this capture. The Tavern is off at Ridgewatch.

The caravan is House Kharûn. This run buys the first goods Kharûn will sell until four units are aboard, and it records the buy and sale figures the market would show. The clock stays at the new-game hour until the camp card. A new game cargo cap is 4 slots and 10 weight. The top bar shows water and rations beside the coin.

36_supplies_hud.png is the market with one water skin aboard, so the top bar reads 1/4 and the water line names the pack. 37_skins_low.png is The Skins Run Low. 38_departure_short.png is You will run dry before Brineford. 39_rescue.png is Dry and the first highlands line. Each of those cards is one frame.

The market panel shows standing with House Kharûn and progress to the next rank. That line stays off the top bar. 42_standing_ranks.png is Rank 5 with 40 / 100 still to go inside the rank. 43_standing_rankup.png is the short toast, Your standing with House Kharûn has risen to rank 1. A rank-up fires that toast. It is not the wood pause panel.

45_tavern_locked.png lists all six pieces with the first one locked. 46_tavern_buy.png shows one lead and 540 coin with a live Buy. 47_tavern_owned.png shows the first piece owned. 48_westmark_gate.png is the Westmark road to Sarn's Rest. 49_sarns_rest.png is the arrival card. 50_end_screen.png is the Embarked ledger.
