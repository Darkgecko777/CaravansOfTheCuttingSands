# Name list

**Status:** Cut this session. Sound rules in `Naming.md`.  
**Size:** Twenty men, twenty women. A save draws once and does not reuse. Add more later under the same test. Do not add a settlement with a vowel swapped.

Houses are named after the cities. No surnames. Given name, then one or two identifiers: son of / daughter of, of [place], vocation. House kin, rare: of House Kharûn.

Men end on a consonant. Women end on a vowel. One or two syllables. Map places are not given names.

The tutorial clerk is fixed and is not drawn: Vekra of Kharûn, the clerk.

Spoken: Thulek son of Korv. Sulena of Draven, the factor.
